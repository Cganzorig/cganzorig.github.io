AI Safety Notes

I will be adding short, actionable posts focused on practical safety questions: what can go wrong, how to detect it, and what mitigation works.

Evaluation first

If a model is safe in a notebook but fails in production, safety is not done. I document measurable checks that keep systems honest before and after release.

Prompt and policy controls

Simple policy layers, strict schemas, and response validation reduce accidental policy drift while preserving useful flexibility.

Data quality defense

Unsafe outcomes often start with unsafe data. I focus on leakage, prompt injection patterns, and robust preprocessing for safer downstream behavior.
